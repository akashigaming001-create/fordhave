My dearest lovelove Dhave,

I don't know how to start this, because every word feels like it's not enough. But I'm starting anyway — because you deserve to know how much I miss you, and how deeply I regret everything that hurt us.

I miss your laugh. The way it would fill a room, the way it made everything feel lighter — like nothing could really go wrong as long as you were laughing. I miss your jokes, even the bad ones. Especially the bad ones. I miss the warmth of your care, the little things you did without being asked. I miss being loved by you.

I know I wasn't always careful with us. I know there were moments when I should have stopped, thought twice, chosen better — and I didn't. I'm sorry. Not the kind of sorry that's just words — the kind that sits heavy in my chest at 2am, the kind that makes me replay things and wish I could go back.

I'm learning. I'm becoming more self-aware — of the things I say, the things I do, the ways I might hurt someone I love without even realizing it. I don't want to be that person anymore. Not for anyone, but most of all, not for you.

If you gave me another chance, I would show up differently. Not perfectly — I can't promise perfect. But present. Intentional. Careful with your heart the way it deserves.

I just miss you, Dhave. So much it's hard to explain. I miss everything.

Always yours, Jinibib
